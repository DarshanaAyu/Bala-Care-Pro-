Data model

Business revenue → direct clinic costs → gross profit → operating expenses → net profit. Owner capital and owner withdrawals are tracked separately. This keeps ROI meaningful. Credit card owed and Gold loan on the home screen are calculated from whichever entries in Debts are tagged that way in the "Show on home screen as" field.
